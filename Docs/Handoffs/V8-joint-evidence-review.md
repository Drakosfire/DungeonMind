## Explicit semantic decisions for review

1. Keep native complete-object reviewed labels/context, assertion ledger and
   completeness. Shared endpoints, direction, admitted facts and exact cited bytes
   are preserved; classic and native ledgers are not universally identical.
2. Keep deterministic native secondary search ordering, admitted seeds first and
   explicit coverage/truncation. Two-node ordering equivalence does not prove
   eight-node or universal secondary-order parity.
3. At a citation cap, choose deterministically among admitted eligible citations,
   disclose truncation and reopen the selected span exactly. The one-anchor witness
   chose native paragraph2 versus classic paragraph1; selection parity is not claimed.
4. Resolve Rules performance as **characterization with no equivalent-work ratio**.
   The native full-input boundary and retained classic representation differ.
   Do not claim matched Rules speed parity. A future Rules consumer needs its own
   matched route qualification. This does not relax the matched Buddy cold gate.
